Professional Property Management

Buying the property is the start. Keeping it tenanted, maintained and performing is the rest of the job.

Why us

Involved from the very start — not handed over at settlement

We can also help if you’re looking to rent out a newly acquired property. Our excellent, cost-effective property management division can be involved from the beginning.

That means rental appraisals on any property you’re interested in — before you buy it, while the numbers still matter — then finding the right tenant, and making sure the property is well managed and kept in pristine condition throughout the tenancy.

Where a property is being acquired as an investment, we ensure the chosen property management agent commences the necessary rental procedures before settlement, so there’s no dead time between taking possession and earning income.

What’s covered

From appraisal to tenancy

Rental appraisals

An honest read on what a property will actually rent for, available on any property you’re considering — not just the ones you already own.

Finding the right tenant

The right tenant is worth more than the highest offered rent. We screen for the tenancy that lasts and leaves the property as it was found.

Ongoing management

Keeping the property well managed and in pristine condition throughout the tenancy — so it holds its value for the whole time you own it.
