Why choose us

The right Buyers Advocate makes all the difference

Our full team has complete knowledge of the markets you’re targeting and a comprehensive approach to property search. Strong relationships with local selling agents, built over many years, sometimes give us access to properties that never come onto the open market. That’s a huge advantage for you.

How we work

Finding your property

Once we have a clear brief, we work as buying agents — liaising with local estate agents and our extensive network of contacts to find and review every suitable property available in your desired areas.

We then visit these properties on your behalf and draw up a short list of the few that best match your requirements, and arrange viewings for all of them. Often this is the only time our clients need to be present during the property search. We’ll keep looking for as long as is necessary — but many of our clients find their ideal property on that single viewing day.

Imagine: no more wasted weekend after weekend in a frustrating property market.

One day Many clients find their property on the first viewing day

On your behalf

Negotiations and due diligence

We conduct all price negotiations for you. In-house or kerb-side auction, sale by undisclosed tender, or a “simple” private sale process — we will be there for your success.

Our detailed area knowledge helps you avoid making an expensive mistake, and gives you peace of mind. With many years of experience we are adept at managing the conveyancing process, and regularly assemble a team of lawyers, financial advisors and surveyors to assist. We keep you abreast of proceedings and communicate promptly and professionally throughout — you stay fully informed at every step.

Completing your purchase

Once we’ve agreed on a property, we conduct all the negotiations for you, securing it for the best price possible and avoiding expensive mistakes.

When your offer has been accepted, we liaise on your behalf with the estate agents, solicitors, surveyors, mortgage lenders and every other party involved — making sure the whole process runs smoothly, professionally and efficiently.

However, it doesn’t stop there

What you get, beyond the purchase

Finding tenants

We can also help if you’re looking to rent out your newly acquired property. Our cost-effective property management division can be involved from the very start — rental appraisals on any interesting property, finding the right tenant, and keeping the property well managed and in pristine condition throughout the tenancy.

Time-saving efficiency

We find you the right property, quickly. No wasting precious time trawling through properties that don’t meet your criteria. All our advice stays relevant to your needs and we only focus on the properties that fit your brief.

Value proposition

Excellent value for money in our service and in the property we help you secure. Our broad range of contacts can give you initial advice on everything from lease extensions, freeholder consents and planning permissions to essential repairs — so you can move from viewing to offer confidently and quickly.

Extensive knowledge

We combine our understanding of your needs with in-depth understanding of the chosen property market to identify the perfect locations and properties within your budget — often finding an ideal solution you may not even have considered.

Your extended team

We coordinate everyone, so you don’t have to

We have strong and longstanding relationships with estate agents, solicitors, surveyors and mortgage lenders. Searching for property is time-consuming, stressful and costly — especially once you understand that the local “friendly” estate agent acts solely for the vendor and landlord, not for you.

Having worked all over the East Coast as buying agents since the mid-’90s, we’ve developed a strong reputation and an excellent track record. Our searches are an estate agent’s priority, and many of the properties we’re offered never come onto the open market.

Once we’ve found your ideal property, you won’t have to deal with the various parties involved in securing your purchase unless you want to. We’ll coordinate and manage the entire home-finding process on your behalf, liaising with every professional on your team, so everything runs as smoothly, efficiently and enjoyably as possible right through to final settlement.

Vendor’s advocacy services

Selling to strengthen your buying position

We can also help you sell your current property, strengthening your buying position. It may be the last thing on your mind — but when the time comes, we’ll be happy to advise on every aspect of the sale.

Selling can be a stressful experience, especially the first time, when you’re not sure of the procedures. There’s a lot of advice out there — friends and relatives who don’t really understand what’s happening. We have unfortunately seen many cases where a homeowner suffered a substantial loss because they listened to the wrong person.

The property market is constantly in flux, so timing matters. Seasonal trends, economic changes and even simple things like school holidays have been shown to affect home sale prices by tens of thousands of dollars.

We’ll support and educate you throughout the process of selling your home using the nine-step framework we’ve developed — from presentation right through to securing a top price, at no additional cost to you as the vendor.
