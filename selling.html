Getting it right

Selling is mostly about timing and advice

Selling your home can be a stressful experience, especially the first time, when you’re not sure of the procedures. There’s a lot of advice out there — friends and relatives who don’t really understand what’s happening.

We have unfortunately seen many cases where a homeowner suffered a substantial loss because they listened to the wrong person.

The property market is constantly in flux, so the first thing to consider is the timing of your sale. Seasonal trends, economic changes and even simple things like school holidays have been shown to affect home sale prices by tens of thousands of dollars.

We’ll support and educate you throughout, using the nine-step framework we’ve developed — from presentation right through to securing a top price, at no additional cost to you as the vendor.
