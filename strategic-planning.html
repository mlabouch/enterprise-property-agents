Strategic Planning

A portfolio is not a pile of properties. It’s a plan that happens to be made of them.

Why us

Analysing, planning and fully strategising your portfolio

We work with clients on a daily basis — analysing and planning and fully strategising their property portfolios, then sourcing, financing and ultimately acquiring the properties that plan calls for.

Most people arrive at us with assets rather than a strategy: a home, some equity, perhaps an investment property bought years ago for reasons nobody wrote down. Strategic planning is the work of turning that into a deliberate position — deciding what to keep, what to release, and what to acquire next.

Three levers

What we look at first

Release equity

The value already sitting in what you own is usually the cheapest capital available to you. We look at what can be released, what it would cost, and whether releasing it actually improves your position — with the right finance and structures around it.

Acquisition of diversified assets

Concentration is the risk most portfolios carry without noticing — one suburb, one property type, one tenant market. We look across residential, commercial, industrial and retail opportunities to spread that exposure sensibly.

Divest non-performing assets

Some properties are quietly costing you the next one. Where an asset isn’t earning its place, we plan the divestment, reposition the capital and reinvest it — acting as your Vendor’s Advocate on the sale so it isn’t given away.

Bringing in the right people

Strategy on paper is worth very little without the specialists to execute it.

We have strong business relationships with trusted allies who are expert qualified financial advisors across all areas of planning and advice, along with the solicitors, accountants, surveyors and mortgage lenders any repositioning is going to need. We’ll happily support you with introductions and sit in on those discussions.
