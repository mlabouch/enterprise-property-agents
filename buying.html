Our buying process

Four steps from brief to keys

Discuss your needs

We listen in detail to your property requirements and the goals you’re trying to achieve. After learning about your needs, we create a plan and strategy for your property.

Search for properties

Many properties are publicly advertised for sale — many are not. Approximately 20% of the properties we purchase are off-market. Our buyer agent professionals have extensive real estate networks, giving you first viewing and first offer.

Research and value

Once a suitable property is identified we conduct due diligence and produce an extensive report: an appraisal of market value, current condition and future appreciation estimates. A negotiation should never commence without this step being done thoroughly, on your behalf.

Negotiate and deliver

Our team implements tough negotiation strategies that put you in the box seat to secure the property. At auction, negotiated before auction or private sale, we strive to get you the best possible price.

Home buyer’s guide

What actually happens, in order

Contact Enterprise Property Agents to set up a Buyer Orientation Meeting and plan your acquisition campaign. Here’s the road from that first meeting to the front door.

  1. Pre-qualify your finance. We’ll work with your current finance sources, or introduce you to a highly qualified, efficient finance broker with access to a wide range of funds from many different secure sources.
  2. Get your structures right. Identify the best structures to ensure your legal and taxation requirements are met. Is your current accountant positioned to help here? If not, we can introduce an expert accounting group for a comprehensive investment plan suited to your circumstances.
  3. Strategise the property. Identify the style, size, location and value that fit your plans.
  4. Commence the search program. Identify, negotiate and acquire the property to suit your convenience and timing.
  5. Due diligence. Find the best property to suit your strategies, and perform all appropriate and necessary due diligence for you.
  1. Negotiate. Secure the best terms and conditions available so you can acquire the property.
  2. Strike a binding contract. Gather all the necessary information to secure the property.
  3. Prepare the logistics. Get ready to move into your new home while the finance and title details are finalised.
  4. Line up management. Ensure the chosen property management agent commences the necessary rental procedures before settlement.
  5. Verify and inspect. Confirm contract details and complete final inspections as required.
  6. Take possession.

Buyers advocacy services

Commercial, industrial, retail and development sites

Analysis & due diligence

Once our teams identify a suitable commercial, industrial, retail or development property, we conduct an intensive analysis of the site with careful due diligence to ensure all matters are fully revealed. The process delivers you an extensive report including value appraisal and development potential.

Negotiation

We bring aboard an exclusive resource to source a huge range of development sites nationally.
