Why us

A logistics background behind every acquisition

Enterprise Property Agents have an extensive background in international and domestic logistics, and therefore real expertise in the identification and acquisition of commercial and industrial property.

We also have a highly experienced property development and acquisitions team able to scour all the best development sites available in the market. We enable our developer clients to access sustainable residential, commercial, industrial and retail opportunities in and around Australia.

Research & value

The process delivers an extensive report including value appraisal and development potential.

Development sites

We bring aboard an exclusive resource to source a huge range of development sites nationally. As a professional developer you appreciate the need to analyse a site’s suitability quickly and acquire it on terms amenable to extended planning and permit processes. Our team is beside you all the way, securing the acquisition concessions your program needs.

Beginners welcome

As a novice or beginner developer you can use our years of experience and contacts to build your own team of trusted professionals. We are with you.

Industrial & commercial

The questions that decide the deal

  • What are your business’s needs, and what will actually satisfy them?
  • What zoning does your business require?
  • What is access like — and what restrictions will you face with it?

Every property presents these questions and in many cases a great deal more. If your ongoing business livelihood depends on getting the move right, you’ll value the assistance.

Once our teams identify a suitable commercial or industrial property to occupy or hold as an investment, we conduct an intensive analysis with careful due diligence so that all matters are fully revealed, and deliver an extensive report including value appraisal — enough for you to decide whether to purchase or lease.

Retail property

Position is the whole game

There’s a myriad of questions when you’re researching the market and looking to secure the best position for a small retail business — whether as a sole trader or as another link in your retail chain.

  • An existing inner-suburban retail strip?
  • A new and evolving suburb?
  • The local boutique shopping centre — or a mega centre?

Whatever your requirements, we can and will find and secure it for you. Once we identify a suitable retail shop or warehouse to occupy or hold as an investment, we conduct the same intensive analysis and due diligence, and deliver an extensive report including value appraisal to support your purchase or lease decision.
