Who We Are

Helping you ease your buying process — saving you time and money.

Our story

Founded in 2009 by people who buy property for a living

We are a professional property brokerage and a Strategic Alliance Partner of The Powerhouse Property Group, originally founded in 2009 by a group of like-minded property investment professionals.

After years of working with clients looking for that special new home, to downsize or relocate, and with investors looking to build successful portfolios, the founders recognised the need for ongoing property investment services — ones that genuinely improve a client’s understanding of the strategies required to build lasting wealth through property.

Enterprise Property Agents provide a full range of strategic property acquisition and advocacy services to clients up and down the Australian East Coast and beyond. We work with clients daily: analysing, planning and fully strategising their portfolios, then sourcing, financing and acquiring quality investment properties — whether to add to a portfolio or to occupy as a principal place of residence.

The founding outcome was simple: give our clients the information, education, skills, strategies, resources and support to succeed in building a successful property portfolio. Since our inception we’ve helped many of our foundation members do exactly that.

Today

A leading East Coast buying agent and property consultancy

Initially established as a local property finder in the mid-’90s, we work to find you — the buyer — the right property to invest or live in, and negotiate on your behalf to get the best terms and conditions, including the best price, without the stress.

A new main residence, a home in the city, a bush or beach escape, a strong rental investment or a development opportunity: we’ll find you the right property in the right area. With deep knowledge of the various markets and first-class research services, we’ll help you into the market and give you the confidence to step forward the moment the right property appears.

Our access to off-market properties, through selling agents in the know, means we can very likely show you properties that aren’t formally listed on the internet at all.

Meet the team

Our extended team

Relationships that put your search at the top of the pile

We have strong, longstanding relationships with estate agents, solicitors, surveyors and mortgage lenders. Searching for property anywhere is time-consuming, stressful and costly — especially once you understand that the local “friendly” estate agent acts solely for the vendor and landlord, not for you.

Having worked all over the East Coast as buying agents since the mid-’90s, we’ve built a strong reputation and an excellent track record. As a result, our searches are an estate agent’s priority — and many of the properties we’re offered never come onto the open market at all.

Time-saving efficiency

We find you the right property, quickly. You don’t need to waste precious time trawling through properties that don’t meet your criteria. All our advice stays relevant to your needs and we only focus on properties that fit your brief — which is why many clients find the perfect property on their first day of viewings.

Value proposition

We aim to deliver excellent value for money — not just in our home-finding service, but in the property we help you secure. Alongside our insight we have a broad range of contacts ready to advise on everything from lease extensions, freeholder consents and planning permissions to essential repairs, so you can move from a viewing to an offer confidently and quickly.

Extensive knowledge

We have a rich knowledge of our marketplaces and a measured, considered approach to property search. Young professional, couple or family buying a first home, upsizing, buying a country or beach getaway, an investor searching for the next purchase or an experienced developer looking for the next big job — we work hard to understand what makes your search unique.
