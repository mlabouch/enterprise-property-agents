Our buying process

Four steps, and we’re with you through every one

Discuss your needs

We listen in detail to your property requirements and the goals you’re trying to achieve, then create a plan and strategy for your property.

Search for properties

Many properties are publicly advertised. Many are not — roughly 20% of what we purchase is off-market. Our networks give you first viewing and first offer.

Research and value

Full due diligence and an extensive report: market value appraisal, current condition, future appreciation estimates. A negotiation should never start without it.

Negotiate and deliver

Tough negotiation strategies that put you in the box seat — at auction, before auction, or private sale — to secure the best possible price.

Vendor’s advocacy — free

Selling too? Get a professional on your side of it.

“Whose side are they on?” is the question we hear most often from sellers. Our Vendor’s Advocacy Service puts an experienced sales manager in the mix who works for you.

  • We recommend the best agency — and the best agent inside it.
  • We audit fees, costs and advertising recommendations.
  • We advise on the strength of any offer you receive.
  • We negotiate on your behalf and keep settlement trouble-free.

And it costs you nothing. Our fee comes from the selling agent, not from you.
