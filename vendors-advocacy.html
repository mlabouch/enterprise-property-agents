Vendor’s Advisory Services

We’ll effectively manage the sale of your property — and the service is free.

Why us

“Whose side are they on?”

That’s the question we hear most often from property sellers — and it’s a fair one.

One of the greatest fears we come across from vendors is the worry of dealing with the selling agency and the agent allocated to handle the sale. Many vendors don’t believe they achieved the best price for their property, and are continually critical of the conflicting advice or the pressure coming from their selling agent.

Our Vendor’s Advocacy Service puts a professional sales manager into the mix who is on your side when you sell your property. Clients welcome the help we provide in finding the right agent, knowing the most appropriate method of sale, identifying an advertising campaign that actually attracts buyers, receiving impartial advice on timing — and having real support through the negotiation.

Our experienced people spend considerable time and energy accurately determining your needs and strategising the sale process for you.

Free Our fee comes from the selling agent — never from you

What we do for you

Enterprise Property Agents is on your side

  • We recommend the best agency — and the best agent within that organisation — to ensure your interests are protected. We know who is good and who isn’t. It’s been our business for years.
  • We audit the fees, costs and advertising recommendations to keep your expenditure effective.
  • We check what sort of job they’re actually doing for you.
  • We peruse and advise you on the strength of any offers you receive.
  • We negotiate on your behalf, keeping you updated all the way.
  • We ensure settlement is as trouble-free as possible.

And the best thing

Our support and assistance is free.

Enterprise Property Agents will do all of this at no cost to you. Our fee comes from the selling agent — we charge them to control the sale for you.

What could be better, if you really have to sell a property, than a professional real estate agent working for you, making sure what you want is what you get?
