Investment Advice & Services

Property is the vehicle. Knowing where you’re driving it is the part most people skip.

Why us

The skills, strategies, resources and support to actually succeed

The founding purpose of Enterprise Property Agents was to provide the information, education and understanding that gives our clients what they need to build a successful property portfolio — not just a single transaction.

We work with clients on a daily basis: analysing, planning and fully strategising their property portfolios, then sourcing, financing and ultimately acquiring quality investment properties to add to those portfolios.

What we work on with you

Four conversations, before any property is bought

Goal setting

We’ll be there with you to set your property investment goals — and to keep them honest as circumstances change.

Wealth creation coaching

Our team and specialist financial planning partners are ready to assist you to implement your wealth creation plans.

Stress management

Life is stressful, and worse when you’re unsure where you’re going. A proper plan breaks that into manageable chunks.

End-game advice

Knowing what waits at the end — when the wealth is built and you want to ease off — is its own challenge. We’ll bring in the right expert.

The practical side

Finance and structures, sorted before you’re bidding

  • Get your finance pre-qualified. We’ll work with your current finance sources, or introduce you to a highly qualified broker with access to a wide range of funds from many different secure sources.
  • Identify the best structures so your legal and taxation requirements are met. If your current accountant isn’t positioned to help, we can introduce an expert accounting group.
  • Build a comprehensive investment plan suited to your specific circumstances — before the search starts, not after.
